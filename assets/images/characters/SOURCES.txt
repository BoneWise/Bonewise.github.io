All images are official-site images, not original artwork by the site owner.
The original rights holders retain their rights; source attribution is not a license.
Files under banner/ and portraits/ are derived from the originals only by trimming
transparent margins, one crop (noted below), resizing and WebP conversion.
Display-time crops and fades are applied with CSS / Canvas.
Full download records (original URL, size, SHA-256) are in materials/kuon-homura/sources.json.
